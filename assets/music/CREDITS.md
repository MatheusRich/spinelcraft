# Music credits

The tracks come from the `mcl_music` mod of [VoxeLibre](https://git.minetest.land/VoxeLibre/VoxeLibre). The files are the VoxeLibre files with no change. When the game plays a track, it changes the loudness so that the integrated loudness is -23 LUFS. The gains are in `game/music.rb`. The authors and the licenses come from the `CREDITS.md` of VoxeLibre.

Each file keeps its license.
